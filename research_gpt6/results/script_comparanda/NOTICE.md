# Source images and scope

Historical manuscript content is used for noncommercial scholarly comparison. Image providers retain their stated terms; source image excerpts are not relicensed as project code.

Yale University Library, Beinecke MS408, f.3r; Huntington Library, San Marino, HM58316, f.1r; München, Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Cod.icon.242, f.1r. Provider URLs, full delivered-raster hashes, native crop coordinates and RGB hashes are in acquisition_receipts.json and regions.json. BSB manifest marks its image Public Domain Mark1.0. Huntington rights information: https://www.huntington.org/library-rights-permissions.

Only small lossless native-context excerpts are preserved for the two external manuscripts; full source rasters are not redistributed. The existing Yale full image is reused unchanged. Each native tile was checked against the delivered full image at acquisition. Offline CI checks stored pixels and reconstruction, not renewed library attribution.

The Fontana sample comes from the alphabet annotation on1r. The date and hand of that annotation were not independently established here. No Latin labels were inferred from its order.

Lisa Fagin Davis2022 lecture is referenced, not redistributed. DejaVuSans.ttf and its license are reused unchanged from the earlier assisted-paleography bundle for captions only. Images show native colours; displayed crops use proportional nearest-neighbor scaling. No rotation, mirror, cleanup or reconstruction is applied.

Two AI visual descriptions do not establish expert paleographic validation, language, cipher equivalence, common author or translation.
